# Credit Memo - Rogers Sugar Inc. (RSI.TO)
*Internal practice memo. Loan request is hypothetical. Figures in CAD millions unless stated; latest fiscal year FY2025, year end late September (FY2025 ended Sep 27 2025).*

## 1. Borrower overview
Rogers Sugar Inc. is a TSX-listed company in Sugar refining and maple products (Consumer Defensive). FY2025 revenue was $1,312.6M and EBITDA $137.8M (10.5% margin). Profile selected for this exercise: Middle (moderate leverage).
- Fiscal 2025 capex was elevated: $95.2M of PP&E additions, of which $74.6M was for the LEAP capacity project (Q4 2025 report PDF p.2)
- LEAP total cost expected at $280-300M, in service in the first half of calendar 2027 (Q4 2025 report PDF p.2)
- $340M revolver maturing March 28 2030; company was in compliance with all covenants at Sep 27 2025 (Q4 2025 report PDF p.31-32)

## 2. Loan request
- Facility: $75M senior term loan - Hypothetical capex term loan (LEAP capacity project; company states total cost $280-300M - FY2025 Q4 report p.2)
- Pricing: CORRA (2.55%) + 2.00% = 4.55% floating (spread: Assumption within disclosed revolver grid of CORRA + 0.20%-2.50% (Q4 report p.31))
- Tenor 5 years; straight-line amortization over 7 years ($10.7M principal per year)
- Size vs EBITDA: 0.54x of FY2025 EBITDA

## 4. Strengths
- Good liquidity: current ratio 1.78x.
- Leverage has improved from 3.67x in FY2023 to 2.81x.
- Revenue grew from $1,104.7M in FY2023 to $1,312.6M in FY2025.

## 5. Risks and mitigants
- Risk: Fixed charge coverage after capex and taxes is 1.26x, leaving little cash after spending.
- Risk: Earnings are uneven: reported EBITDA in FY2022 differs from yfinance's normalized EBITDA by more than 20% (unusual items); that year is not used as a trend baseline.
- Risk: About 38% of existing debt is floating rate (see Assumptions), plus the new loan.
- Mitigant: Proposed maintenance covenants (below) give the bank an early warning and a seat at the table if results weaken.
- Mitigant: Scheduled amortization over 7 years reduces exposure each year.
- Mitigant: Because a covenant breaches under stress, conditions below are required before approval.

## 7. Proposed covenants
- Maximum Debt/EBITDA (incl. leases): 4.00x
- Minimum EBITDA/Interest: 3.00x

| Scenario | Debt/EBITDA headroom | Coverage headroom |

*Headroom = how much EBITDA could fall from that scenario before the covenant breaches.*

## 8. Recommendation
**Approve with conditions** - internal rating 6 standalone, 7 pro forma, 8 under combined stress (1 = strongest, 10 = weakest).
- Basis: pro forma rating 7 is in the watch range (5-7); at least one proposed covenant breaches under stress.
- Conditions: quarterly compliance certificates; covenants as above tested quarterly; cash sweep of 50% of excess cash flow while Debt/EBITDA is above the covenant less 0.50x; no increase in distributions while a covenant has less than 15% headroom.
