# Credit Memo - Richelieu Hardware Ltd. (RCH.TO)
*Internal practice memo. Loan request is hypothetical. Figures in CAD millions unless stated; latest fiscal year FY2025, year end Nov 30.*

## 1. Borrower overview
Richelieu Hardware Ltd. is a TSX-listed company in Specialty hardware distribution (Industrials). FY2025 revenue was $1,964.0M and EBITDA $213.9M (10.9% margin). Profile selected for this exercise: Strong (low leverage).
- Funded bank debt is small: $9.9M of long-term debt at Nov 30 2025; the rest of total debt is $244.2M of lease obligations and a $22.9M bank overdraft (AR 2025 PDF p.59 and p.67)
- Spent $47.1M on nine business acquisitions in fiscal 2025 (AR 2025 PDF p.58)
- Has authorized lines of credit of $85M and US$56M (AR 2025 PDF p.58)

## 2. Loan request
- Facility: $100M senior term loan - Hypothetical acquisition term loan (general corporate / tuck-in acquisitions)
- Pricing: CORRA (2.55%) + 1.50% = 4.05% floating (spread: Assumption (Richelieu's US line is SOFR+1.40%, AR p.58))
- Tenor 5 years; straight-line amortization over 10 years ($10.0M principal per year)
- Size vs EBITDA: 0.47x of FY2025 EBITDA

## 4. Strengths
- Low leverage: Debt/EBITDA of 1.29x (including leases) in FY2025, 1.76x after the new loan.
- Strong interest coverage: EBITDA covers interest 14.9x.
- Good liquidity: current ratio 3.29x.
- Free cash flow (OCF - capex) equal to 68% of total debt in one year.
- Revenue grew from $1,802.8M in FY2022 to $1,964.0M in FY2025.

## 5. Risks and mitigants
- Risk: Leverage has risen from 0.92x in FY2022 to 1.29x.
- Mitigant: Proposed maintenance covenants (below) give the bank an early warning and a seat at the table if results weaken.
- Mitigant: Scheduled amortization over 10 years reduces exposure each year.
