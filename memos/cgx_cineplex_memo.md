# Credit Memo - Cineplex Inc. (CGX.TO)
*Internal practice memo. Loan request is hypothetical. Figures in CAD millions unless stated; latest fiscal year FY2025, year end Dec 31.*

## 1. Borrower overview
Cineplex Inc. is a TSX-listed company in Movie exhibition and entertainment (Communication Services). FY2025 revenue was $1,284.8M and EBITDA $251.8M (19.6% margin). Profile selected for this exercise: Weak (highly levered).
- Shareholders' deficit of $79.5M at Dec 31 2025 (FS PDF p.9)
- Existing debt: $575M 7.625% senior secured notes due 2029 and $216.25M 7.75% convertible debentures due 2030 (FS PDF p.36)
- $100M covenant-lite revolver maturing March 4 2027, undrawn at year end (FS PDF p.36-37)
- Lease principal repayments of $89.8M in 2025 on top of interest (FS PDF p.13)

## 2. Loan request
- Facility: $60M senior term loan - Hypothetical capex term loan (theatre refurbishment)
- Pricing: CORRA (2.55%) + 4.25% = 6.80% floating (spread: Cineplex's actual revolver margin on CORRA advances (2025 FS PDF p.37))
- Tenor 5 years; straight-line amortization over 5 years ($12.0M principal per year)
- Size vs EBITDA: 0.24x of FY2025 EBITDA

## 4. Strengths
- No ratio meets the strength thresholds used in this memo.

## 5. Risks and mitigants
- Risk: High leverage: Debt/EBITDA of 7.12x (including leases), 7.36x after the new loan.
- Risk: Thin interest coverage: EBITDA covers interest only 1.78x.
- Risk: Weak liquidity: current ratio 0.52x (current liabilities exceed current assets).
- Risk: Low free cash flow relative to debt: (OCF - capex)/Debt of 5.1%.
- Risk: Fixed charge coverage after capex and taxes is 1.44x, leaving little cash after spending.
- Risk: Negative shareholders' equity of -$79.5M; lenders have no equity cushion on the balance sheet.
- Risk: Leverage has risen from 5.89x in FY2023 to 7.12x.
- Risk: Revenue fell from $1,388.9M in FY2023 to $1,284.8M in FY2025.
- Risk: Earnings are uneven: reported EBITDA in FY2022, FY2024 differs from yfinance's normalized EBITDA by more than 20% (unusual items); those years are not used as a trend baseline.
- Mitigant: Covenants and amortization would not offset the weaknesses above; see recommendation.

## 7. Proposed covenants
- Maximum Debt/EBITDA (incl. leases): 7.50x
- Minimum EBITDA/Interest: 1.50x

*Headroom = how much EBITDA could fall from that scenario before the covenant breaches.*

## 8. Recommendation
**Decline** - internal rating 10 standalone, 10 pro forma, 10 under combined stress (1 = strongest, 10 = weakest).
- Basis: pro forma rating 10 is at or above the decline line (8); DSCR falls to 0.81x under combined stress (below 1.00x).
- The bank could revisit if leverage falls below the decline line on audited results, or with additional collateral / a guarantor that changes the risk.
